Strategic Alignment

Choose Influencers Based on Audience Relevance, Not Follower Count Alone

A creator with a large following is not automatically the right choice for every campaign.

The value of an influencer partnership depends on whether their audience, content and communication style match the brand's objectives.

Before selecting creators, important considerations include:

Audience location

Whether the creator reaches people in the locations your business wants to target.

Content category

Whether their regular subjects are relevant to your product or service.

Audience quality

Whether the available audience information and engagement patterns appear credible.

Content quality

Whether the creator communicates naturally and produces content that suits your brand.

Language and culture

Whether the creator's language and communication style connect with your intended audience.

Campaign suitability

Whether the creator's usual content formats can support your campaign requirements.

For businesses targeting Trichy, a creator's connection with the local audience may be more valuable than a large audience spread across unrelated locations.

Similarly, a brand targeting Tamil Nadu may benefit from evaluating Tamil-language creators and regional audience relevance.

The objective is to identify a suitable brand-creator match rather than select influencers based on popularity alone.

Tailored Campaign Models

Influencer Marketing Campaigns That Support Different Business Goals

Every collaboration should have a clear reason behind it. The content format and creator selection should reflect what your business wants to communicate.

Product Promotion and Demonstration

Introduce products through creator-led videos, demonstrations, sponsored posts and relevant product experiences. This format helps audiences understand what a product offers through content created for the selected platform and audience.

Brand Awareness Campaigns

Collaborate with relevant creators to introduce your business to audiences who may not already know your brand. Campaigns can focus on brand introductions, product categories, company announcements and other relevant brand messages.

Product Launch Campaigns

Build awareness around a new product or collection through coordinated creator collaborations. The campaign can use product introductions, demonstrations, launch announcements and other agreed content formats.

Sponsored Content and Creator Collaborations

Work with creators to develop brand-related content that fits their established communication style. Clear briefs, approved product information, deliverables and publishing expectations help keep the collaboration aligned with the campaign objective.

Reviews and Experience-Based Promotions

Creator reviews and product experiences can communicate how a product or service works. Any claims made in promotional content should be accurate and supported. Creators should not be asked to present invented experiences or unsupported product benefits as genuine opinions.

Giveaways and Audience Engagement Campaigns

Giveaways and creator-led collaborations can introduce a brand to participating audiences. Campaign conditions, eligibility, promotional messaging and responsibilities should be clear before publishing.

Measurement & Accountability

How Campaign Performance Should Be Evaluated

A campaign should be evaluated against the objective established before it begins.

For example, a brand awareness campaign and a lead-generation campaign require different measurements.

Campaign objective Relevant performance indicators
Brand awareness Reach, impressions and video views
Audience engagement Likes, comments, shares and saves
Website visits Tracked link clicks and landing-page visits
Enquiries Form submissions, calls and attributable enquiries
Product promotion Product interactions, tracked purchases and conversions where available

Campaign reports should distinguish between views, reach, engagement and actual business conversions.

For a more meaningful assessment of commercial performance, campaign costs and attributable outcomes should also be considered. A high view count alone does not establish revenue or return on investment.

The appropriate measurement approach depends on the platform, available tracking information and the campaign's original objective.

Compliance & Ethics

Responsible Influencer Advertising Builds Better Brand Communication

Sponsored collaborations should be communicated transparently.

In India, ASCI's influencer advertising guidelines require disclosure when a material connection exists between a brand and influencer. This can include monetary compensation, free products, discounts, gifts and other benefits.

Appropriate disclosure labels include Ad, Sponsored, Collaboration and Partnership, depending on the arrangement.

The disclosure should be visible, prominent and understandable to the audience. Brands and influencers also share responsibilities concerning advertising claims and the appropriate disclosure of commercial relationships.

RiverBird's campaign planning should account for these requirements when coordinating sponsored creator content.

For further guidance, refer to the ASCI Influencer Advertising Guidelines and ASCI Influencer Advertising FAQs.
