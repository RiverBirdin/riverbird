Our Timeline

The Riverbird Story

From a freelance passion project to a growing digital solutions company, Riverbird's journey is built on innovation, entrepreneurship, and creating opportunities for businesses and future professionals.

2024

The Beginning of Riverbird.in

Riverbird.in started as a freelance venture by ambitious corporate professionals. Working on client projects after office hours, the team transformed their skills into a successful side business. As the venture grew, it generated greater opportunities than their full-time careers.

2025

Launching as a Digital Marketing Agency

Two founders officially established Riverbird as a digital marketing agency in their hometown. With a focus on creativity and measurable business growth, the company began delivering digital marketing, branding, and web development solutions to local businesses.

2026

RiverBird Digital Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

Riverbird became RiverBird Digital Solutions Pvt. Ltd., expanding into a growing team of 10+ professionals. The company also created 15+ internship opportunities, helping aspiring marketers gain real-world experience while supporting businesses with innovative digital solutions.
