Everything you need to know about our brand identity services.

A complete brand identity package includes your logo suite (primary, secondary, monogram), colour palette with hex & CMYK codes, typography system, brand voice guidelines, usage rules (do's and don'ts), and a brand style guide PDF. We also deliver social media profile assets and business stationery templates ready to use.

We collaborate closely at three key stages — brand discovery (understanding your vision, audience, and competitors), concept presentation (we share 2–3 distinct directions), and refinement (feedback rounds). Beyond those touchpoints the work is hands-off for you. Most clients invest 2–3 hours of focused time across the whole project.
