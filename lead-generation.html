How It Works

A Connected System From First Click to Follow-Up

Individual lead tools work best when aligned in a synchronized 6-stage pipeline.

Stage 1: Visitor Interest

Targeted traffic arrives on custom campaign landing pages from Google Search, Meta Ads, or organic SEO channels.

Stage 2: Offer & Value

The page clearly communicates the service or downloadable asset, addressing objections and establishing trust.

Stage 3: Enquiry Capture

Visitors submit essential contact and project requirement fields through friction-free responsive forms.

Stage 4: Lead Screening

Automated rules evaluate submissions against your target criteria (geography, budget, company size) to score lead readiness.

Stage 5: CRM Integration

Captured lead data syncs instantly into your CRM pipeline (HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho) for your sales team to act upon.

Stage 6: Email Nurturing

Automated, personalised email sequences warm up prospects, share case studies, and trigger scheduled sales calls.
