Selection Process

How Our 3-Stage Hiring Process Works

We keep our recruitment transparent and respectful of your time. Every application submitted receives human evaluation within 3 to 5 business days.

Stage 01

Screening & Review (3–5 Days)

No automated screening bots. Our recruiting team personally evaluates your CV and portfolio, then schedules an intro call to align on experience, goals, and work preferences (on-site or hybrid).

Stage 02

Practical Skill or Campaign Review

Engineering applicants undertake a focused architecture or coding task; marketing candidates review a real campaign or audit exercise; interns complete a learning-readiness task.

Stage 03

Conversation with Lead

Meet directly with the department lead to discuss everyday workflows, team expectations, compensation or stipend details, and target onboarding timelines.

Common Questions

Careers & Internships FAQ

Everything you need to know about applying, interview stages, and working with RiverBird in Tiruchirappalli.

How do I apply for digital marketing, software engineering, or internship roles at RiverBird?

You can apply directly using the Apply Now form on this page by entering your full name, email address, target position, and cover note, and uploading your resume in PDF or DOCX format (maximum one file per submission). Alternatively, you can email your CV and portfolio links to info@riverbird.in with the exact role title in the email subject line.

Does RiverBird hire freshers and offer internships in Trichy with full-time conversion potential?

Yes. We actively run internship tracks—including our Web Engineering Intern role and Digital Marketing Internships—alongside select entry-level openings. Our internships give students and fresh graduates practical experience on real business projects rather than classroom theory, and strong performers are considered for conversion to full-time positions.

Are RiverBird jobs based on-site in Tiruchirappalli, hybrid, or remote?

We offer on-site and hybrid roles based out of our headquarters in Melachinthamani, Tiruchirappalli (and select roles in Chennai), as well as remote options within India depending on the specific position. Each job listing specifies its work mode, and you can note your location preference in your cover note when applying.

How long does it take to hear back after submitting an application, and what is the interview process?

Our recruiting team reviews every application personally and typically responds within 3 to 5 business days of receiving your CV. The interview process consists of three stages: an initial screening call, a practical skills assessment or portfolio/campaign review, and a final conversation with the hiring lead.

I am an employer looking to hire staff for my company—should I use this Careers page?

This Careers page is for candidates applying to join RiverBird's internal engineering, marketing, creative, and internship teams. If you are a business owner or HR leader looking to hire vetted developers, designers, or volume operational manpower for your own organization, please visit our Staffing Solutions hub, our Hire Talents page, or our HR & Manpower Services page, or reach our client desk via our Contact page.
