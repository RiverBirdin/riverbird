Methodology

Our 5-Step Graphic Design Workflow: From Brief to Final Files

A structured workflow ensures your design project progresses with predictable timelines, clear feedback milestones, and no surprises:

1

Step 1: Creative Brief & Objective Alignment

We discuss your project goals, target audience, brand guidelines, preferred reference styles, delivery deadlines, and final output specifications (print versus digital).

2

Step 2: Content, Copy & Visual Asset Intake

You share your copy, high-resolution logos, product photography, and any mandatory regulatory text (such as FSSAI numbers or barcode specs). If you need assistance with copywriting, our in-house team drafts compelling headlines and body copy.

3

Step 3: Concept Layout & Wireframe Exploration

Our designers explore typography pairings, visual composition, and colour palettes—presenting 1 to 2 distinct creative directions for major collateral or initial layout proofs for review.

4

Step 4: Iteration & Stakeholder Revisions

We refine the chosen layout based on your feedback. Standard projects include up to three structured revision rounds to fine-tune spacing, hierarchy, and colour balance.

5

Step 5: Final Production Artwork & Multi-Format Delivery

Once approved, we prepare production-ready files: print-ready PDFs with correct bleeds, web-optimised PNG/JPG exports, and organized vector source files (AI, PSD, or Figma) transferred with full intellectual property ownership.

Pricing Factors

What Determines the Cost of Graphic Design Services?

Every commercial project is scoped transparently in Indian Rupees (INR, ₹) based on:

  • Deliverable Format & Complexity: A single social media creative has a different production scope than an intricate 3D packaging dieline or a multi-page corporate brochure.
  • Copywriting & Asset Creation: Whether text and photography are supplied upfront, or if your project requires copywriting, custom vector illustration, or image retouching.
  • Page Count & Size Adaptations: Designing a 12-page technical catalogue or adapting a master campaign visual across six different social and print dimensions requires more layout work than a single-page poster.
  • Revision & Turnaround Requirements: Up to three revision rounds are included in standard project scopes; additional revision rounds can be added at a nominal, pre-agreed cost.
