FAQ

Digital marketing questions, answered clearly.

Practical guidance before you choose a channel, budget or engagement model.

Which digital marketing channel should our business start with?

The right starting point depends on your most urgent gap. If customers cannot find you on Google, begin with SEO and a clear mobile website. If you need enquiries quickly for a specific offer, admission period or sales season, paid Google and Meta campaigns paired with a focused landing page provide faster initial data. We audit your current presence first and recommend the highest-priority channel for your budget.

Should we invest in paid advertising or SEO?

Paid advertising creates immediate targeted visibility, making it useful for rapid lead generation, offer testing and retargeting. SEO takes longer to build momentum, but its organic visibility compounds without a fee for every click. Many growing businesses use paid ads for near-term enquiries while building search authority in parallel.

How much does digital marketing cost, and how is it billed?

Investment depends on the number of channels, creative production volume and market competition. We provide a customized proposal in Indian Rupees with applicable GST details. SEO, social management and advertising optimization usually run as monthly retainers; branding, website builds and funnel setup can be fixed-scope projects. Advertising media spend paid to Google or Meta is shown separately from management fees.

How long does it take to see meaningful results?

Timelines vary by channel and starting point. Paid campaigns, landing-page improvements and automated follow-up can begin collecting data soon after launch, followed by continuous optimization. Technical SEO, content and organic community building develop progressively over several months as pages are indexed and audience trust compounds.

How do you track leads and campaign performance?

We configure conversion pixels, analytics dashboards and structured landing forms before scaling campaigns. This lets us monitor impressions, clicks, conversion rates and acquisition cost accurately. Enquiries can also sync directly into your CRM and trigger automated email follow-up, helping your sales team respond to every warm lead.
