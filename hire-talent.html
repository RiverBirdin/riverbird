Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about IT staffing, contract-to-hire models, and onboarding timelines with RiverBird in Trichy.

1. How does IT staff augmentation work compared to contract-to-hire and direct placement?

In IT staff augmentation (contract staffing), a pre-vetted developer, designer, or marketer embeds into your daily workflows for a specific project or monthly term while you direct their tasks without adding permanent headcount. Contract-to-hire starts with a contract period so you can evaluate real sprint performance before converting the specialist to your internal payroll under pre-agreed terms. Direct placement recruits a permanent employee straight onto your company's payroll from day one.

2. What types of technical and digital roles can we hire through RiverBird in Trichy?

Through this page, you can hire software and web developers (Python/Django, Node.js, React, PHP, full-stack, and Shopify/e-commerce), mobile application developers, UI/UX and graphic designers, video editors, digital marketing specialists (SEO, Google & Meta Ads, social media, and lead generation), and technical project coordinators. For high-volume operational, retail, logistics, or shift-based staffing, please use our HR & Manpower Solutions service.

3. How quickly can you share a candidate shortlist and complete onboarding?

We aim to respond to all new staffing enquiries within 24 hours on business days. For standard web development, UI/UX design, and digital marketing roles where vetted professionals are available in our active pool, we work toward delivering an initial shortlist within 48 hours and completing onboarding in under two weeks. Highly specialized architectures, multi-round client interviews, or full-time candidates serving existing notice periods may require additional time, which we outline clearly during your discovery call.

4. What happens if a placed developer or specialist is not the right fit after joining?

Every engagement includes a dedicated recruiter who monitors early onboarding and gathers feedback from your team. If a placed professional does not align with your technical or workflow expectations during the initial onboarding phase, we provide replacement support under a defined replacement window set out in your written staffing agreement.

5. How are IT staffing and contract-to-hire engagements priced, and what details do you need for an estimate?

Engagements are scoped and quoted in Indian Rupees (INR) with GST details (unless another currency is agreed in writing for international clients). Pricing depends on whether you choose monthly contract augmentation, contract-to-hire, or one-time permanent placement, alongside the required tech stack and years of experience. To get an accurate estimate, share the role title, required tools or languages, seniority level, work location (Trichy on-site, hybrid, or remote), expected start date, and budget range via our Contact page.
