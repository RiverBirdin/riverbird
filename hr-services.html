Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about operational manpower services, volume hiring, and compliance workflows in Trichy.

1. What is the difference between operational manpower services and specialist technical recruitment at RiverBird?

Operational manpower services on this page focus on volume recruitment, identity and reference verification, batch onboarding, shift scheduling, and payroll coordination for retail, logistics, hospitality, manufacturing support, and administrative roles. For single-role or specialist technical hiring—such as software engineers, UI/UX designers, and product managers tested for code quality—we provide a dedicated technical screening track on our Hire Talents page.

2. How are fees structured for manpower recruitment services in Trichy?

Commercial models depend on your headcount volume, role complexity, and engagement duration. We offer both monthly retainer models and project-based volume recruitment structures. All proposals and estimates for Indian clients are provided in Indian Rupees (INR) with applicable GST details after we review your staffing brief.

3. How does RiverBird verify candidates before onboarding?

Every candidate goes through a structured screening workflow before batch onboarding. This includes screening against your role and shift criteria, verifying government identity documents, reviewing prior work history logs, and conducting reference checks so only vetted personnel are scheduled for deployment.

4. Can RiverBird handle urgent or high-volume hiring across multiple shifts?

Yes. Our sourcing and scheduling workflows are built for multi-shift and bulk operational requirements. We capture your shift patterns, holiday coverage needs, and site locations in the initial staffing brief, process applications in batches, and provide ongoing attendance and shift reporting. Urgent staffing enquiries can also be escalated directly by calling +91 99949 67655.

5. Do you only provide manpower services in Trichy, or can you support teams in other cities?

While our headquarters are located in Melachinthamani, Tiruchirappalli, we support employers across Trichy, throughout Tamil Nadu, and on a pan-India basis. You can coordinate with our recruitment team remotely via phone or email, or schedule an in-person consultation by appointment at our Tiruchirappalli office.
