Our Web Development Process

A disciplined, 4-stage engineering lifecycle — ensuring every project is delivered on schedule, fully tested, and ready to scale.

STEP 01

Discovery & Strategy

We analyze your business objectives, map target user journeys, define tech architecture, and establish clear scope milestones.

STEP 02

UI/UX Wireframing

Crafting high-fidelity interactive prototypes, responsive layout grids, and design systems aligned with your brand identity.

STEP 03

Vanilla Engineering

Building performant HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript code. Integrating APIs, headless CMS backends, and payment gateways.

STEP 04

QA & Cloud Launch

Rigorous cross-device testing, Core Web Vitals optimization, SSL setup, and seamless deployment to global CDN edge servers.
