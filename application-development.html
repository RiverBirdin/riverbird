Frequently Asked Questions

Mobile Application Development FAQs

Practical answers about choosing platforms, budgeting, app store publishing, integrations, and ongoing maintenance.

1. How do we know if our business needs a mobile app or just a mobile-friendly website?

If customers only visit your business occasionally to check information or send a one-off enquiry, a fast, mobile-responsive website is usually more cost-effective. A dedicated mobile application is worthwhile when customers or staff use the tool repeatedly—such as regular ordering, loyalty rewards, course access, or daily field reporting—or when your workflow requires push notifications, offline access, camera scanning, or GPS tracking.

2. Should we build a cross-platform app (Flutter or React Native) or separate native Android and iOS apps?

For most business, e-commerce, booking, and startup applications, cross-platform frameworks like Flutter or React Native are the most practical choice because a single codebase powers both Android and iOS, reducing build cost and simplifying future updates. We recommend native Kotlin (Android) or Swift (iOS) when an application depends heavily on specialized device hardware, complex background tasks, or OS-specific system integrations.

3. What determines the cost of mobile application development in Trichy, and how are projects quoted?

The cost depends on the number of user roles (e.g., customer app, field/partner app, and admin dashboard), the complexity of the backend database, third-party integrations (such as payment gateways or existing software), and whether you need Android, iOS, or both. After a discovery discussion, we provide a written proposal in Indian Rupees (INR) with applicable GST details, available either as a fixed-scope milestone project or a dedicated engineering team model.

4. Do you handle Google Play Store and Apple App Store submissions, and can the app connect to our existing software?

Yes to both. During planning, we review the API documentation of your current website, CRM, ERP, or accounting software to design secure data synchronization. At launch, our team prepares the production builds, configures store listings and privacy declarations, and manages the submission and review process for both the Google Play Store and Apple App Store.

5. What happens after our app is published, and why does a mobile app need ongoing maintenance?

Mobile operating systems (Android and iOS), third-party payment SDKs, and app store compliance rules update every year. Without periodic maintenance, an app can experience crashes on newer phones or face store removal. We offer ongoing post-launch support covering server and crash monitoring, OS compatibility updates, security patches, and phase-two feature enhancements.
